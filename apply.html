Apply for the Accelerated Academic Award Scholarship

The application asks for your name, your class year, your email address, and an essay of at least ten words. Read the essay requirements before you begin. All fields are required.

Part 1 of 3Applicant information

The year you expect to graduate from the school you currently attend.

Part 2 of 3The Ten-Word Essay

Prompt

In ten words or more, describe the future you are working toward.

Minimum ten words. No exceptions.An essay under ten words is disqualified immediately and will not be read.

  • There is no maximum length, but longer essays do not score higher for being longer.
  • Hyphenated words, contractions, and numbers count as one word each.
  • No title. Everything in the box below counts toward your total.
