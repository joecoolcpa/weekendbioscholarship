2026–27 Award Cycle

Accelerated Academic Award Scholarship

Each cycle, WeekendBio awards the Accelerated Academic Award Scholarship to one student for the strongest essay submitted in response to its prompt. The recipient receives the full award of $1.39, paid entirely in U.S. pennies.

Selection is based on the essay alone. There is no minimum GPA, no test score requirement, and no application fee.

The Ten-Word Essay

The essay is the only part of the application that is scored. It must contain at least ten words. An essay under ten words is disqualified immediately and is never read.

Plan to spend far more time on it than on the other three items combined. Applicants who begin drafting early have more room to revise.

Read the full essay requirements

2026–27 Essay Prompt

In ten words or more, describe the future you are working toward.

Minimum ten words. No exceptions.An essay of nine words or fewer is disqualified immediately. It will not be read, scored, or returned for revision.

There is no maximum length. Hyphenated words and contractions count as one word each.

The award

The full $1.39 goes to a single recipient. It is not divided among finalists, spread across semesters, or paid in installments. The recipient receives all 139 coins at once.

The award is paid only in pennies. It will not be converted to a check, a gift card, a bank transfer, or larger coins. Together the coins weigh roughly 350 grams, or about 12 ounces.
